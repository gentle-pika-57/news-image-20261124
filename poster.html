TECH  ·  SANTA CLARA  ·  ①

NVIDIA BRINGS BACK HUANG AS CEO

Jensen Huang reinstated as president, CEO and director on 23 Nov, just 11 days after resigning over an affair scandal. Trump said he brokered the deal. Shares surged about 31% on Monday, regaining roughly $1 trillion in value.

2
WAR  ·  BENGHAZI

UKRAINE SINKS RUSSIAN WARSHIP OFF LIBYA

Seven sea drones sank the corvette Merkury at Benghazi naval base on Sunday; a cargo ship and a Ka-27 helicopter were also hit.

3
CLIMATE  ·  ANTALYA

COP31 COLLAPSES OVER FOSSIL FUELS

Talks ended Sunday with no deal — the first major COP breakdown since 2009 and the longest COP ever. The draft omitted fossil fuels.

4
CRIME  ·  GUASAVE, SINALOA

US DRONE HELPS NAB MEXICO DRUG LORD

“El Chapo Isidro” captured on 20 Nov; Mexico confirmed US surveillance drones helped pinpoint him. Twenty died in the operation.

5
MARKETS  ·  NEW YORK · SEOUL · TOKYO

WALL STREET ROARS BACK AS ASIA RALLIES

Nasdaq jumped 6.5% and the S&P 500 rose 4.7% on Monday, best day since April 2025; KOSPI +7.5% and Taiwan +9.1% followed.

6
RIGHTS  ·  QOM, IRAN

IRAN FLOGS SINGER FOR HIJAB-FREE SHOW

Parastoo Ahmadi, 29, and four crew were each flogged 74 times on Monday for a 2024 online concert without a hijab.

7
WAR  ·  DYMER, KYIV REGION

KYIV REGION STRIKES KILL AT LEAST 14

Ten died in Dymer, three in Vyshneve and one in Bila Tserkva after overnight strikes; rescuers hunted for survivors in rubble.

8
CHINA  ·  BEIJING

CHINA DEATH SENTENCE IN SPY CASE

Official surnamed Ma sentenced to death for leaking secrets: over 100 reports sent, 35 classified, for over 1m yuan.

9
AID  ·  NEW DELHI

INDIA GIVES NEPAL $1.62BN FLOOD AID

$377m grant plus a $1.24bn credit line after Modi–Shah talks, for recovery from August floods that killed over 1,300.

10
STRIKE  ·  BRUSSELS

BELGIUM STRIKE GROUNDS EVERY FLIGHT

All flights at Brussels airport cancelled 07:00–21:00 on Monday as a national anti-austerity strike hit transport and schools.

11
CONFLICT  ·  KHARTOUM

DRONES STRIKE UN SITE IN KHARTOUM

Drones hit homes and a UN building early Sunday, killing at least four including a 10-year-old; Sudan blamed the RSF.
